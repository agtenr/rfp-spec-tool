## Observation
Stories drafted from a rich upstream design document (here the HLD) tend to fail objective criterion O5 (dependencies named or explicitly "none") even when the source document clearly states a hard dependency — because the dependency lives in the referenced doc (HLD: hard dependency on Instrument 1, BR-16) and is not restated in the story body. The cause is that referencing a design doc for scope does not carry its dependency declarations into the story text, so "silence is a miss" fires on otherwise well-grounded stories.
